# Everlife Open: One Health × SDGs × bounded computation

**Status: formal research prototype.** No human or animal immortality, clinical benefit, ingestible GSH capsule, physical zero entropy, universal halting solution, or AI consciousness-preservation has been demonstrated.

## Machine-readable public registry

Source verifier: [registry.py](registry.py). Public JSON: [public/latest.json](public/latest.json). AI discovery: [public/llms.txt](public/llms.txt).

On any machine with Python 3:

```sh
cd research/everlife-open
python3 registry.py
```

The source independently exhausts 4,096 stationary policies for both finite and renewable resource scenarios of a **synthetic 12-state** GSH/GSSG-inspired model. A maximal robust safe invariant kernel of 12 for replenishment and zero without replenishment is a mathematical statement about this toy model, **not** proof of indefinite survival. It separately verifies reversible G257 and ATGC *symbolic* encodings and a finite U2 involution; no physical mechanism is inferred.

## Research scope and SDGs

One Health research includes **human, cat and dog** wellbeing, plus animals and ecosystem health. Align thematically with SDGs 3, 9, 12, 13, 15 and 17; record **no official indicator outcomes** until separately measured. Each species requires its own evidence and veterinary/clinical oversight. No prescription, dosing, tests on living animals, or 3D-printed ingestible formulations are authorized here.

## Diet, cognition and odor (proposed, no health records)
The [public diet/cognition/odor research proposal](nutrition_cognition_odor_study.json) and [fail-closed metadata reviewer](nutrition_study.py) evaluate whether hypotheses about meat consumption, reading clarity/brain fog, axillary odor, breath odor and bowel odor are adequately distinguished from confounding variables. A separate **withdrawal-like diet transition** hypothesis is recorded as an untested question; food reward and habit changes are possible but there is no validated meat-specific withdrawal syndrome. "High-frequency spiritual vibration" is an unoperationalized personal interpretation, not an established physiological explanation.
The additional logical BF8 virtual job in [unified_registry.json](unified_registry.json) proves only the finite scheduling and reversible representations, not nutritional or medical findings. The review JSON is generated by CI as nonpersonal metadata and, if separately deployed, would be available read-only at `/v1/nutrition-study`. No personal records are accepted, no diagnostic advice or supplement dosing is generated, no human/animal dietary intervention is conducted, and no real-time observation is claimed. Any eventual participant research requires voluntary informed consent, privacy review, clinician/researcher oversight, risk assessment and suitable ethics approval.

## Ethical protein transition: plant foods, fermentation, cultivated meat
Research-only [protocol](ethical_protein_transition.json) and bounded [evaluator](ethical_protein.py) study alternatives to slaughter, including adequate vegetarian/vegan diets, microbial and precision-fermentation protein, and cultivated meat. The design explicitly audits donor animal welfare, lethal/embryonic cell sources, FBS/serum, all upstream animal-derived components, scaffold/growth factor inputs, risk of creating pain-capable organized nervous tissue, approved food safety, and independently sourced lifecycle costs. It **never** certifies zero suffering or human cognition improvement from a computer simulation. Each ethical criterion must be evidenced independently.

No meal/fasting intervention is initiated, no culture procedure is performed, no individual health data are stored in Git, no high-dose supplements are recommended, and no product is claimed authorized for sale. The seventh and eighth BF8 virtual jobs are finite *synthetic metadata indicators*, not real dietary trials, cell cultures or market-ready alternatives. Standalone public read-only `/v1/ethical-protein` would need an isolated authorized deployment; it is NOT LIVE on this research branch. The source is evidence-labeled and should be revised as new peer-reviewed and regulatory evidence emerges.
